Known Limitations

Performance degrades slightly in heavy fog or extreme low-light without IR enhancement. Highly dense smoke occlusion may cause bounding box fragmentation. Recommended deployment pairs this with acoustic heat/CO sensors for multi-modal fallback verification.

Production-grade features ready out-of-the-box

CPU/GPU Routing

Dynamic device allocation. Automatically quantizes to FP16 on CUDA or falls back to OpenVINO/CPU threads maintaining ~50ms latency.

Django Channels

Handles 50+ concurrent WebSocket streams. Async consumers push base64 frames and JSON coordinates in real-time.

Smart Alerting

Configurable confidence thresholds per class. Triggers SMS/Email/PagerDuty via Celery beat schedules.

Active Learning

Auto-captures low-confidence frames for human review. One-click dataset export for seamless continuous retraining.
